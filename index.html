VEM UTILITY

About Us

VEM Utility by Vinay Dagriya is designed as a practical digital toolbox for electrical, machinery and workshop calculations.

The goal is simple: replace repeated manual calculations and searching through tables with quick, easy-to-use tools that work on your phone.

We are starting with SWG wire calculations, unit conversion, bearing identification and tap-drill selection, with more engineering utilities planned.

More details about VEM Utility and the team can be added here later.
